Vijaykumar Shantilal Tadvi vs. State of Gujarat decided

the learned trial Judge has to play a very little role while
dealing with the bail plea in a case where the accused was
asked to undergo punishment for a period less than three
years and the amount of fine imposed by the trial Court is paid.
In that very fact situation, even the appellate Court normally
should not refuse the bail. The Court is conscious that bail is a
rule and jail is an exception is no longer a good law, but certain
categories of cases obviously would fall in the class where the
refusal of bail can be equated with denial of legitimate
freedom of personal liberty even in absence of presumption as
to innocence.
