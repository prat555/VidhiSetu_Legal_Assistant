                      -:J U D G M E N T:-

     The plaintiff law firm has filed this suit against

defendant No.1 to 3 seeking for relief of          permanent

injunction against the defendants,         claiming under her
                              4          O.S.No.2781/2021

not to interfere with    the day to day practice of the

plaintiff's Law firm.



2. The factual matrix of plaintiffs' case is as under :

  The plaintiff Law firm's associates practicing before the

Hon'ble High Courts and Hon'ble Supreme Court, they

have filed number of cases and other public interest

litigation. The defendants are accused in Cr.No.2/2021

before the CID Cyber Crime Police, Bengaluru. Few

accused persons in Cr.No.8/2021 before the CID, Cyber

Crime Police, Bangalore are clients of the plaintiff and the

plaintiff law firm has filed numbers of cases represented

the accused persons before the Sessions Court, Hon'ble

High Court of Karnataka and Magistrate Courts all over

Karnataka and outside Karnataka also.
                              5           O.S.No.2781/2021

3.   One Devaraj who is utter stranger to the Plaintiff

Law Firm and their associates, on 06.08.2020 at about

4.15 pm one Devaraj tresspassed into the law firm of the

plaintiff and started abusing in filthy language to plaintiff

associate and showed knife, when the acts of abusing

and showing knife was questioned by the plaintiffs

associate,   at that time, he has given life threat and

attempted to stab the plaintiff associate. In this regard,

the plaintiff lodged complaint against him before the

Cubbon Park,Police Station.      Accordingly, the Cubbon

Park police have registered the case in Cr.No.63/2020 for

the offence punishable U/s.307, 441, 504, 506 of
IPC
.

Further, plaintiff Law Firm and filed suit against the said

Devaraju before the CCH-36, Bengaluru against Devaraju

and obtained interim order on 26.08.2020.
                               6          O.S.No.2781/2021

4.    The defendant No.3 who is accused in Cr.No.8/2021

and he is in J.C under UTP.No.5691/2021. The plaintiff

Law Firm filed Writ Petition on behalf of the defendant

No.3 same was disposed off. Even though the defendant

No.3 is in J.C. he started frequently calling the plaintiff

Law Firm Associates through phone call and mobile call.

On 08.12.2021 at about 03.39pm, defendant No.3 called

the   plaintiff     Law   Firm,   through   mobile    phone

bg.No.8951907741 and threatened the plaintiff Law Firm

dire consequences.



5.    Immediately, the plaintiff Firm lodged the complaint

against the defendant No.3 before the Directorate General

of Police (Prison) Inspector of General of Police (prison)

and    the   Jail    Superintendent   (Prison).      Due   to

apprehension of threat and interference in day to day
                              7           O.S.No.2781/2021

activities of the Law Firm, the plaintiff has filed this suit

against the defendants for the aforesaid relief.



6.   Summons to defendant and also issued notice to

defendant No.3 through Superintendent jail, Bengaluru.

Inspite of service of notice to defendant, he failed to

appear before this Court. Hence, defendant placed ex-

parte. Then, case is posted for evidence.



7.   In order to discharge the burden lies on the plaintiff,

one Mohammed Kamran Khan is examined as PW.1. He

has produced 19 documents, same are got marked at

Ex.P.1 to Ex.P.19.



8.   Heard the learned counsel for the plaintiff.



9.   The following points arise for my consideration:
                             8           O.S.No.2781/2021



     1)   Whether the plaintiff Law firm is entitled for
          the relief as sought for.?


     2)   What order.?



10. On considering the plaint averments, oral evidence

of PW.1 and documentary evidence and hearing of the

parties, my answer to aforesaid issues as under;

          Issue No.1 : In the Affirmative
          Issue No.2 : As per final order
                                 ----------------for following


                         REASONS

11. The learned counsel for the plaintiff submitted that,

the plaintiff Law Firm having branch office at Bengaluru,

Hyderabad and Delhi and practicing before the Hon'ble

High Courts and Hon'ble Supreme Court of India. The
                               9        O.S.No.2781/2021

defendant No.3 is accused in Cr.No.8/2021. The plaintiff

Law Firm filed application on behalf of the defendant

No.3 before the Hon'ble High Court of Karnataka in Writ

Petition. Same was disposed off. But the defendant No.3

started threatening the plaintiff Law firm by phone calls.

In this regard, the plaintiff Law firm lodged complaint.

This fact reveals from the documents produced by the

plaintiff. Hence, the plaintiff proved the facts and

allegations made against the defendant. Hence, prays to

grant permanent injunction as sought for.



12. PW1 stepped into the witness box and filed affidavit

in lieu of examination in chief, wherein he has reiterated

the complaint averments. Therefore, it is not necessary

to elicit the same facts herein.
                               10          O.S.No.2781/2021

13. This Court perused the documents produced by the

plaintiff's Law Firm. It clearly discloses that the plaintiff's

office Firm is situated at the address stated in the cause-

title of the plaint. Further, Ex.P.7 discloses that after

receipt of the threat phone call from the defendant No.3,

immediately, they have lodged the complaint before the

Prison authority. Further another important document

i.e., Ex.P.18 discloses that Jail authority immediately

responded to the complaint lodged by the plaintiff's Law

Firm. By considering all these aspects, it clearly discloses

that the defendant No.3 threatening        and interfering in

the practice of the plaintiff's Associates. Therefore, the

plaintiff's Law Firm is entitled for grant of relief as sought

for.   Accordingly, Point No.1 is answered in the

Affirmative.
                                  11               O.S.No.2781/2021



14. Issue No.8:

For the foregoing reasons, I proceed to pass following.

                             :ORDER:
The suit of the plaintiffs is hereby decreed.
Further,      the        defendants     and    their
      claimants or any body claiming under them are
      herby    restrained       by     granting      permanent
      injunction restraining from interference in the
      day to day practice of the plaintiff's Law Firm by
      granting permanent injunction.
