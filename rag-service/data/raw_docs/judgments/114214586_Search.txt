11. Thus it is held by the Hon'ble Supreme Court

         in
Thirumoorthy
(supra) that trial of a CICL shall be

         conducted under the provisions of the
Juvenile Justice (Care

         and Protection of Children) Act, 2015
and not under the
POCSO Act
.
Patna High Court CR. APP (SJ) No.443 of 2025 dt.17-07-2025
16/18
12. In view of aforesaid decision passed by the

         Hon'ble Supreme Court, this Court has no other alternative

         but to hold that a CICL involved in a case under the
POCSO Act
shall be dealt with either by the Juvenile

         Justice Board or by the Children's Court as per the

         provision of the
Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of

         Children) Act, 2015
.
13. In view of
the above decision
, this Court takes

         up the instant appeal for determination.
14. The appellant was arrested in connection with

         Basopatti P.S. Case No. 45 of 2022, dated 25 th of February,

         2022, instituted for the offences punishable under
Sections

         363

15. On the date of occurrence, it is held by the

         Juvenile Justice Board that the appellant was above 16

         years of age but below 18 years. Therefore, trial of the

         appellant shall be conducted by the Children's Court.
16. It is alleged that the victim had voluntarily

         eloped and married the appellant and was living with him.
 Patna High Court CR. APP (SJ) No.443 of 2025 dt.17-07-2025
17/18
She is now pregnant.
17. The appellant is in custody since 5 th of

         September, 2024.
18. Though the appellant is minor, this Court deals

         with the instant appeal on the presumption of innocence of

         the appellant, because the victim voluntarily eloped with

         him and both of them married and she is now pregnant.
19. In view of such circumstances, I am inclined

         to release the appellant on bail.
20. The appellant, Nitish Paswan, is directed to be

         enlarged on bail on furnishing bail bonds of Rs. 25,000/-

         (twenty five thousand) two sureties of the like amount each

         to the satisfaction of learned Additional Sessions Judge 1 st

         of the bailor must be either of the parents of the appellant,

         under the care and protection of his parents and under the
