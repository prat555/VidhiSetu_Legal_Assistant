Act: Indian Penal Code, 1860
Section: 420
Heading: Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property

Section 420 IPC states that whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces the person deceived to deliver any property to any person, or to make, alter or destroy the whole or part of a valuable security, is punishable.

Punishment:
Imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven years, and also liable to fine.
